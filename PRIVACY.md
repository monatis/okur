# Okur's privacy policy

Okur is a screen reader. It reads what is on your screen aloud, and it runs entirely on your
computer.

## What Okur collects

Nothing. Okur sends no data anywhere: it has no telemetry, no analytics, no accounts, and it
makes no network connections.

## What stays on your computer

To read your screen, Okur reads the text and controls of the windows you use, through Windows'
accessibility interfaces, as every screen reader does. What it reads is spoken with the voices
installed on your computer and is not stored.

Okur keeps on your computer only what you set: its settings, voice profiles, PlaceMarkers and
the like, in its own folder of your user profile. It also keeps a small log of its own warnings
and errors there (at most about 2 MB), for when you report a problem, and a fuller one only
when you turn that on. Its logs stay on your computer; Okur never sends them. Uninstalling Okur
removes what the Microsoft Store version kept.

Text recognition (OCR) uses Windows' own recognizer, on your computer.

## Changes

If a later version of Okur adds a feature that sends anything off your computer, it will be off
until you turn it on, it will say what it sends and where, and this policy will say so before
that version is published.
