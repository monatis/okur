City Library Expands Evening Hours

By Maria Lopez, published March 14, 2026

The Riverside City Library will stay open until 9 p.m. on weekdays starting in April, the board announced on Tuesday. The change follows a survey in which most respondents asked for later hours.

What changes

Membership costs $5.99 a month, e.g. for adults; children join free. Questions go to help@riverside-library.org — or call 555-0142. Attendance rose 3.5% last year, and staff say they can't wait.

  • Weekday hours: 9 a.m. to 9 p.m.
  • Weekend hours, which do not change:
    • Saturday: 10 a.m. to 6 p.m.
    • Sunday: noon to 5 p.m.
  • New study rooms open in May.

What readers said

Finally, I can study after work.

The library board will review attendance in the fall. A long-standing complaint among students and working parents was that the reading rooms closed before most people finished their day, so the extended schedule is intended to make the collections, the public computers, and the quiet study spaces available at the times when the community actually needs them, according to the library director.
