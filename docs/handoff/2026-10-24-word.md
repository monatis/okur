## What was built

### Word's documents, read as JAWS reads them

The platform reads the caret line's structure from Word's UI Automation text (`13e3595f`,
`LineStructure` in `crates/okur-core/src/event.rs`): Word's own line, the paragraph's heading
level (style ids 70001-70009) and list, and the table cell (its place, its table's number, its
column's header and its row's first cell). Only Word's document window (`_WwG`) is read so.
`crates/okur-core/src/apps/windows/word.rs` words it as JAWS does:
- a heading's level before its line, "heading level 1 Library Hours", on arrows, Ctrl+Home and
  Ctrl+End, Say Line and Say Focus;
- a list entered and left after the line, "Entering List", "exit list";
- a table entered, changed and left before it, "table 1 | uniform Table | new cell | Branch",
  "new cell | Central", "out of table";
- Ctrl+Home or Ctrl+End that leave the caret on its line say nothing.

### Word's tables

- JAWS's table keys move the caret through UI Automation (the grid's item, its text range
  selected): Ctrl+Alt+arrows say the title for the way the cell was entered (the column's
  header across a row, the row's first cell along a column), else the cell's column or row;
  Ctrl+Alt+Home and End say the table's edge, both titles and the cell; at an edge, "end of
  row", "top of column"; outside a table, "Not in a Table".
- Tab is Word's own and adds "Selected"; the last cell warns that Tab adds a row.
- Say Cell (Ctrl+Alt+Numpad 5) and Say Focus give both titles, the cell, its column and row, and
  for the focus the table.

### Word's Heading List, Say Font and Say Window Title

- Insert+F6 lists the document's headings, read paragraph by paragraph by their styles, in the
  Heading List a page has; Move To Heading moves the caret by paragraph and says the line, then
  "Edit"; Escape says "Edit".
- Say Font says the size, the colors, the font, the paragraph's style, its alignment and its
  outline level. JAWS also says a line spacing that Word's text does not report: a known-gap test,
  `say_font_says_the_line_spacing`.
- Say Window Title adds the view, "Print View", from the status bar's view button that is on.
