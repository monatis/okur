## What was built

### Word

- **Say All by paragraphs** (`46398a48`, `fabdf199`). In Word's document Say All asks the
  platform for the document's paragraphs: each one's start, heading level, list and table cell,
  read through Word's text. It then reads from the caret's paragraph on, saying before each
  paragraph what changed, as JAWS does [obs, 1038/s4, s12]:
  "What changes | Entering List | Weekday hours are longer. | ... | exit list | heading level 2
  Branches | table 1 | uniform Table | Branch | Hours | Row 2 | Central | 9 to 9 | out of table |
  The page ends here."
  - Line reading says the list after the line, but Say All says it before.
  - Say All says no "new cell", and "Row 2" at a table's next row.
  - JAWS's own order varies from trial to trial. Okur follows two trials of three.
  - Without the paragraphs (an older trace, or no Word), Say All reads by lines, as before.
- **The view keys** (`adc8ebfc`): Ctrl+Alt+N, Ctrl+Alt+O and Ctrl+Alt+P say "Draft View",
  "Outline View" and "Print View" [obs, 1039]. Word's status bar marks neither Draft nor
  Outline, so the view a key chose is also the one Say Window Title says.
- **Say Font** (`adc8ebfc`) says "bolded", "italicized" and "Single  underline" before the size
  [obs, 1040/s3, s5, s7].
- **Table titles** (`9e997540`), from 1040 and what Word reports (`word-probe.ps1`):
  - a column's title is its first-row cell, header row or not;
  - a row's title is the row's first cell only where Word reports row headers (the table's first
    column marked); otherwise "Row 1 no title";
  - Say Cell on the first row says "This is the title row, containing column titles." first;
  - "Selected" comes right after the cell;
  - Tab on to the next row says the row's place, then the column's.
  1040 was recorded again with this tracer (`323266cc`), and its table steps match.

### Words read on their own (`f1e3307a`)

- The virtual cursor's Ctrl+Right, Ctrl+Left and Say Word give the synthesizer every symbol
  named, whatever the punctuation level: "month comma,", "period.", "dog comma," [obs, 106/s4,
  s6; 213/s11].
- A word that is one symbol is said as that character alone: "em dash —" [obs, 106/s23].
- Naming every symbol now keeps the voice's text processing: mixed case, numbers digit by digit
  with "point", dollars left to the voice [obs, 721-723]. A selection's naming had used the
  defaults.
- Spoken layer, compared step by step with before: 9 steps fixed, none broken.

## Harness

## Found, and how

- **Word reports no line spacing through UI Automation.** `word-probe.ps1` read every text
  attribute (40000 to 40042) of paragraphs at 1.08, 1.5 and double spacing, and 40040
  (LineSpacing) is not among them. JAWS says "Line spacing: 1 lines.", "one point five
  lines.", "Double" [obs, 1040/s1, s17, s19], 1034's two steps, and its heading's "0 lines.".
  The known-gap test stays (`say_font_says_the_line_spacing`).
- **Word's row and column headers follow the table's marks.** 1034's table (header row and
  first column marked) reports both; 1040's reports neither. JAWS still takes column titles from
  the first row.
- **Excel's recording failed** (521, three trials): the Office Activation Wizard took the focus,
  and the keys went to its buttons. The evidence was not imported (`9ae179a0`). Excel started
  by the fixture is still running on old-pc with the wizard open: close it, then record again.
- **1038's replay begins a line ahead** at s1 and s2. Trial 1's trace shows the caret already
  moved twice before s1, though JAWS's speech for s1 is the second paragraph; after s5's
  Ctrl+Home every line is right. Replay takes trial 1's trace. To look at in the recorder or
