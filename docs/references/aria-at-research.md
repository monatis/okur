# ARIA-AT: what it holds, and how Okur records it

Research for [`../plans/2026-10-31-aria-at-web-round-1.md`](../plans/2026-10-31-aria-at-web-round-1.md),
2026-10-31. Sources: the repository <https://github.com/w3c/aria-at> at commit
`6c0044502636c8fa6452579f6e10a0d7ca80b3b1` (2026-10-06), its `tests/` folder read directly; the
reports at <https://aria-at.w3.org/reports>, read through the report app's GraphQL API
(`https://aria-at.w3.org/api/graphql`, its schema in
<https://github.com/w3c/aria-at-app/blob/main/server/graphql-schema.js>); the licenses at the
W3C's pages named below.

## The test plans

`tests/apg/` holds 40 plans, one per pattern of the ARIA Authoring Practices Guide (APG);
`tests/aria/` and `tests/html/` hold 3 more. Two formats:

- **The second format** (28 of the APG plans): in `data/`, `tests.csv` (`testId`, `title`,
  `setupScript`, `instructions`, `assertions`, the assertions' ids), `jaws-commands.csv`
  (`testId`, `command`, `settings`, `assertionExceptions`, `presentationNumber`),
  `assertions.csv` (`assertionId`, `priority` 1 for MUST and 2 for SHOULD, the statement and its
  phrase), `references.csv` (the page, the pattern, the ARIA attributes), `scripts.csv` (the
  setup scripts). NVDA's and VoiceOver's commands sit in their own files.
- **The first format** (12 plans: banner, breadcrumb, the two comboboxes, complementary,
  contentinfo, form, main, menubar-editor, meter, rating-slider, slider-multithumb):
  `commands.csv` with a column per command and an `at` column, modes `READING` and
  `INTERACTION`. Not read by Okur's importer yet.

In the second format the APG plans hold **859 JAWS commands**: 540 in the virtual cursor
(`virtualCursor`, "virtual cursor active"), 319 in the PC cursor (`pcCursor`, "PC cursor
active", reached by Insert+Z: `tests/support.json`). Their keys, by `tests/commands.json`'s
tokens: letters (quick keys `b`, `f`, `x`, `t`, `u`...), `ins`, `shift`, `ctrl`, `alt`, the
arrows, `home`, `end`, `pageUp`, `pageDown`, `tab`, `enter`, `space`, `esc`, digits by name
(`nine`). A command of several presses is written with spaces ("down down").

**Test pages.** `references.csv` names the page, `reference/<date>/<example>.html`, an APG
example with its `css/` and `js/`. Next to it, one page per setup script
(`<example>.<setupScript>.html`, generated by ARIA-AT's `process-test-directory.js`) adds the
setup script and, as the page's last element, a "Run Test Setup" button (autofocus) that runs
it when activated: moving the focus to a link before or after the widget, onto it, or changing
its state. A test's instructions start from that state ("With focus on the 'Navigate forwards
from here' link, navigate to the 'All condiments' checkbox.").

## The reports

The report app's final reports (`testPlanReports(isFinal: true)`) for JAWS, all on Chrome, give
per plan the MUST and SHOULD assertions met. Of 49 JAWS reports, three are of **JAWS
2025.2505.43**, old-pc's build: accordion (MUST 198/200, SHOULD 48/48), disclosure-faq
(129/129, 36/36) and radiogroup-roving-tabindex (206/206, 154/167). The others are of JAWS
2025.2508.120 (the newest), 2025.2503.39, 2024.2310.70, 2023.2307.37 and 2021.2111.13. Plans
with assertions JAWS missed, by the newest report: accordion (2 MUST), disclosure-navigation (4
MUST), minimal-data-grid (13 MUST, 24 SHOULD, JAWS 2021 only), quantity-spin-button (1 MUST, 5
SHOULD), switch-checkbox (4 MUST), and SHOULD gaps in the menu buttons (14 of 81), the radio
groups and tabs. Per command, each report holds the tester's transcript of JAWS's output: leads
for what JAWS says, never evidence (AGENTS.md §3).

### A newer JAWS in the reports

The reports hold what newer builds of JAWS say than old-pc's (2025.2505.43): most plans have a
report of **JAWS 2025.2508.120**. `skills/jaws-reference/scripts/aria-at-reports.sh <json>` saves
every final report's outputs from the report app's GraphQL API (`https://aria-at.w3.org/api/graphql`,
no sign-in), and `okur-lab aria-at-report --newer-jaws <json>` lines up, for each imported
scenario, the newest JAWS's output for its plan, test, command and setting beside the recorded
JAWS's and Okur's, listing where the two JAWS differ. On 2026-11-01: 142 of 792 commands. Most
were the testers' settings or timing (a hint said before the group, no group on a quick key);
what a newer JAWS does on purpose, Okur follows (accepted divergences, "following a newer JAWS"):

- a slider's and a spin button's limits after a number, "Red left right slider | 128 min 0 max
  255", "Adults edit spin box | 1 | min 1 max 8"; none after a text value (the seek slider's
  "1 Minute 30 Seconds of 5 Minutes"), where JAWS 2025.2505 says them; a spin button's not on
  Insert+Up;
- the hints of a menu button, a slider, a spin button and a dialog's first field with the
  virtual cursor on, not only with it off;
- "Modal" before a modal dialog with the virtual cursor on;
- a page menu's item and its place on Insert+Tab and Insert+Up with the virtual cursor on,
  "Action 1 | 1 of 4", not "Action 1 menu";
- an ARIA grid's name once on Tab, not again before the cell.

Not followed: Insert+Up on a menu button without its state (JAWS 2025.2505 says "collapsed",
which ARIA-AT asks for), and a radio button's hint with the virtual cursor on (said before the
group in some outputs and after it in others; on old-pc's forms pages JAWS says none).

## How Okur records them

`okur-lab aria-at-import --tests <checkout>/tests --plan apg/<plan> --first-id <n> --source
<commit>` (`crates/okur-lab/src/aria_at.rs`):

- copies the plan's `reference/<date>/` folder unchanged to `aria-at/<plan>/` (a fixture in a
  folder: the corpus keys it by its path, its snapshots are named with dots for slashes);
- makes a setup page the plan's folder lacks (7 plans lack some) as ARIA-AT's build does: the
  setup script before `</head>`, the button before `</main>`
  (`lib/data/example-scripts-template.js`);
- writes a scenario per JAWS command: its fixture the test's setup page; its reset `Ctrl+End`,
  `Shift+B`, `Enter`: JAWS's Ctrl+End lands on "main region end", Shift+B goes back to the
  page's last button, the setup's, and Enter runs the setup, after which JAWS says the page's
  title and the focused element, with the virtual cursor on it (old-pc, 2026-10-31; a first
  round with Ctrl+End and Enter only pressed nothing and was stopped); then `Insert+Z` for the
  PC cursor; its steps the command's presses, then `Insert+Z` again in the PC cursor, so
  that the next scenario starts in the virtual cursor; its `aria_at` the plan, test, command,
  setting, assertions and exceptions. Ids from 2000.
- Each trial reloads the page (Ctrl+R) before the reset, so the setup runs afresh.

## The measure of assertions

`okur-lab aria-at-report` (`crates/okur-lab/src/aria_at_report.rs`) replays the ARIA-AT
scenarios and judges each assertion twice, on Okur's speech and on JAWS's first recorded
transcript, the same way: the command's steps joined (a PC cursor scenario's closing Insert+Z
left out); a role by the words JAWS uses for it ("check box", "radio button", "TabPanel"); a
state by its words, whole ("checked" is not in "not checked"); a name as written, letters and
digits only ("cant" is "can't"); a place as "2 of 3"; "some or all" of a text by its first six
words. An assertion it cannot read (a list's boundary, the ability to edit text) is unjudged.
Priority 1 is MUST, 2 SHOULD, 3 MAY; a command's exceptions lower an assertion's priority
(the tab list's role is MAY for JAWS's commands).

A negative assertion, "not convey numeric value, '25.0'", is met when the number is said only
within its text ("25.0 degrees Celsius"), never bare.

Its figures are a judge's, read by hand before they are trusted: the unmet MUST assertions of
2026-11-01 were all real, and JAWS leaves the same ones (an accordion header's heading level
on the PC cursor's Tab; Shift+Tab into a radio group with none checked landing on its first
radio button, the page's tab stop). Where Okur conveys more than JAWS (56 assertions then), it
is the places JAWS leaves out (accepted divergences and the open steps of rating and tabs).
